Layer 1: Machine Learning Demand Forecasting

Multi-step recursive forecasting using Gradient Boosting Decision Trees (GBDT)

🤖

Why Autoregressive Lags & Multi-step Recursive Forecasting?

If a material has a lead time of 7 days, an order placed today will not arrive for 7 days. Our GBDT model creates autoregressive lags (t-1, t-7, t-14, t-30) and rolling windows (mean 7/30d, std 7d) to predict daily consumption recursively over the entire lead-time window.

Layer 2: Mathematical Stock Optimization

Mathematical formulations for Safety Stock ($SS$), Reorder Point ($ROP$), and Economic Order Quantity ($EOQ$)

Safety Stock Formula

Safety Stock ($SS$)

$$SS = z \times \sigma_{90} \times \sqrt{L}$$
    $z = 1.645$ (95% Service Level z-score) $\sigma_{90}$: 90-day historical usage standard deviation $L$: Replenishment lead time in days
Reorder Point Formula

Reorder Point ($ROP$)

$$ROP = (\bar{d}_{\text{forecast}} \times L) + SS$$
    $\bar{d}_{\text{forecast}}$: Predicted mean daily usage from Layer 1 Triggers purchase alert moment stock drops below $ROP$
Economic Order Quantity

Economic Order Quantity ($EOQ$)

$$EOQ = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times (365 \cdot \bar{d}) \times S}{H}}$$
    $S = \$50$: Fixed order procurement cost $H = \$2$: Annual holding cost per unit
